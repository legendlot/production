Packaging (PKG) is the final assembly-line step: confirm the car and remote are a pair, choose the sales channel, and print the dispatch label that the unit carries from here on.

Station
PKG · lines L1 to L5.
You scan
Car first, then remote.
Who
Operator
Offline
Needs a live connection.

The flow

  1. Choose the channel first. Tap Ecom or Retail at the top. Your choice sticks until you change it, and it decides which label prints.
  2. Scan the car UPC. For a unit with a remote, a purple band asks you to scan the remote next.
  3. See “Pair Verified, Label Ready”. The overlay shows the batch label (LOT-XXXXXXXX-E or -R), the channel, and that the print job is queued.
  4. Tap Next Unit to continue (or Reprint Label if needed).
⛔ Set the channel before you scan

The channel toggle decides whether an Ecom or Retail label prints, so check it is right before scanning. While the “Label Ready” overlay is up, further scans are ignored: tap Next Unit to move on. As at QC Pass, scan the car before the remote, and use Cancel to abandon a half-finished pair.
