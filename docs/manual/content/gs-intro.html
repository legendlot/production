The Scanner is the handheld app that records the physical work on the production floor. At every station, an operator scans a barcode and the system updates instantly: a unit is inwarded, passed, packed, dispatched. This guide walks through every station. This first chapter covers the things that are true everywhere, so learn it once and the rest is easy.

What it’s for
Recording floor work, scan by scan.
Runs on
An Android device at each station.
One device
One station, one line, per shift.
Where
scanner.legendoftoys.com

Starting the camera

The camera does not start on its own. Every time the scan screen opens you will see a Tap to start camera button: tap it, then hold the barcode in the middle of the yellow frame. Holding the same code twice within two seconds does nothing (the scanner ignores rapid duplicates).

What a scan tells you

Every station gives the same three signals, so you always know what happened without reading the screen closely:

ResultWhat you see and hear
SuccessGreen flash, a rising triple beep, a short buzz, and a green tick line in the log. The scan worked.
RejectRed flash, a falling buzz, and a full-screen red REJECT panel with the reason and “RESCAN THE CODE”. It clears itself after a moment.
Pending / warningOrange flash and an orange message: the scan was counted but something needs your attention (for example an over-pick, or a scan queued while offline).
⚠ Working offline

If the device loses connection you will see an orange OFFLINE bar and a queued count. Many scans (Assembly, QC Fail, Outsourced Inward, Restock) are saved and sync automatically when the connection returns. But the stations that need live data: QC Pass, Packaging, Pack, Store Issue, Repack, Workshop and the dispatch stations, will reject with “OFFLINE” until you are back online.

ⓘ Each station expects its own code

A station only accepts the barcode it is meant for. Scanning a box label at a unit station (or the wrong-format code anywhere) gives a WRONG BARCODE reject. If you keep getting rejected, check you are at the right station for the label in your hand.
