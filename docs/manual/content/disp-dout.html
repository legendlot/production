Dispatch Out (DOUT) is the final exit scan as goods leave the building. It accepts a whole box or a single unit.

Station
DOUT · lines D1 / D2.
You scan
A box label or a batch label.
Who
Operator
Sign in
Scan your QR card per shift.

The flow

Scan a box label (BOX-XXXXX) to send the whole box out (“Box out, N units”), or a batch label (LOT-XXXXXXXX-E/R) to dispatch a single unit (“Dispatched Out”). The channel is shown on success.

ⓘ One station, two label types

Dispatch Out is the only station that takes both a box label and a unit label, so you can ship a packed box in one scan or send out a loose unit. Anything else gives “Invalid label”.
