Restock returns unused units to stock: orders that were cancelled, unsold inventory, sample returns. It is not for units that went out with a courier and came back; those go through Returns Intake.

The flow

  1. Pick the reason (Order Cancelled, Unsold Inventory, Sample Return, or Other). The reason sticks across scans.
  2. If you pick Other, type the required reason note.
  3. Scan the unit’s LOT UPC. The unit goes back into stock.
⛔ Courier-touched returns do not belong here

The panel reminds you: a unit that physically shipped and came back must go through Returns Intake (RTO In), not Restock. Restock is only for units that never left in a customer order. If you pick “Other” you must enter a note, or the scan is rejected.
