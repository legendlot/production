Allocate (ALLOC) commits units to a specific sales channel (a particular Amazon, Flipkart, retail partner and so on) before they are packed into a shipment.

Station
ALLOC · lines D1 / D2.
You scan
Batch labels (after picking a channel).
Who
Operator
Sign in
Scan your QR card per shift.

The flow

  1. Select the channel first. Choose the channel from the dropdown at the top.
  2. Scan each batch label. A green tick confirms “Allocated to [channel]” (or “Re-allocated” if it was on another channel).
⚠ Pick the channel before scanning

If you scan before choosing a channel you get NO CHANNEL, select a channel above before scanning. Set it once and it stays selected for every unit you allocate to that channel; change it when you switch channels.
