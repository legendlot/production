The flow

Scan the batch label (LOT-XXXXXXXX-E or -R). A large channel badge flashes Ecom or Retail, and the Session Tally at the top counts up by Ecom, Retail and Returns. Undamaged returns coming back through show as “Return” and count separately.

ⓘ Scan the right label

PKG Out expects a batch label with its channel suffix. A unit UPC or any other code gives “WRONG BARCODE, expected LOT-XXXXXXXX-E or -R”. This is a dispatch-floor station, so the operator signs in with their QR card at the start of the shift.
