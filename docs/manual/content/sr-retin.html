Returns Intake (RET_IN) receives units coming back from customers and couriers. It is a two-step scan: first the shipping label to identify the channel, then each returned unit, with a disposition that says what happens to it next.

Station
RET_IN · shared.
You scan
AWB first, then unit labels.
Who
Operator
Modes
Shipment or Individual.

The flow

  1. Scan the AWB / shipping label. This resolves the sales channel. If it cannot match one, a red picker asks you to choose the channel by hand.
  2. Set the disposition for the unit you are about to scan: UDR (good, restockable), Repair, or Loss. For Repair and Loss you also pick the box condition; for Loss you must type a note (for example “empty box”).
  3. Scan each unit’s batch label. It is logged with its disposition. In Shipment mode the AWB stays set for many units; in Individual mode it clears after each.
  4. Close the shipment with “Done, close shipment” when finished.
ⓘ This is the courier-return path

Any unit that physically shipped and came back belongs here, not at Restock. A returned label has its -E / -R handled automatically. A “Loss” disposition always needs a note, and the unit’s disposition here is what routes it to the UDR pool, the repair pool, or a loss write-off in Garage.
