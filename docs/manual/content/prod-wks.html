Workshop is where failed units are received for repair and later sent back out. You do not pick “in” or “out”: the scanner works out which one this is from the unit’s history.

Receiving a unit (first scan)

The first scan of a unit shows the Workshop In overlay with the product, its loop number, and the list of defects logged at QC. Tap Unit Received, Scan Next to take it in.

Sending a unit out (next scan)

Scanning the same unit again shows Workshop Out with the defects that were meant to be repaired, and asks for the outcome:

  • Repaired, send to QC (green): the unit goes back for another quality check.
  • Cannot fix, scrap (red): the unit is written off.
ⓘ The two scans are the two steps

There is no mode to set: the first scan takes a unit in, the next scan of that same unit sends it out. The overlay tells you which step you are on, so just follow the on-screen buttons.
