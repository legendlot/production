Pack puts allocated units into shipment boxes. It works two ways: packing into a named shipment, or packing direct to a channel.

Shipment mode

  1. Select the shipment from the dropdown (it shows the channel and how full it is).
  2. Open or pick a box. For bulk channels, pick an open box (or the box bar warns “No open boxes”, in which case boxes are added from the dashboard). For per-unit channels, each scan auto-creates and closes a box.
  3. Scan batch labels to add units to the open box; Close the box when full to print its label.

Direct mode

Pick a channel, then each scanned batch label auto-creates a box, packs it, closes it and prints, all in one go. Use this when you are not packing into a pre-built shipment.

ⓘ What the rejects mean

“No shipment” or “No channel” means you have not made the selection at the top yet; “No open box” means you need to open or add a box first. As a dispatch station it needs a live connection and your shift QR sign-in.
