QC Pass marks a unit as having passed quality control. For products that ship with a remote, it also verifies that the car and its remote are a genuine pair before letting them through.

Station
QC_PASS · lines L1 to L5.
You scan
Car first, then remote.
Who
Operator
Offline
Needs a live connection.

The flow

  1. Scan the car’s LOT UPC first. The hint says “Scan LOT UPC (car first)”.
  2. If the product has a remote, a green band appears: “Now scan remote control”. Scan the remote to confirm the pair. A single-piece product passes straight away.
  3. See “Paired” and the green tick. The unit is QC-passed.
⚠ Always car first, and watch for PAIRING FAILED

Scan the car before the remote. If you scan a remote that does not belong to that car you get PAIRING FAILED: find the correct remote and scan again, or tap Cancel to abandon a half-finished pair and start over. This station needs a live connection, so it rejects with “OFFLINE” if the device is offline.
