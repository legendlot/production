QC Fail records a unit that did not pass, along with exactly which defects were found. The unit then goes to the workshop for repair.

The flow

  1. Scan the unit’s LOT UPC. An orange flash fires and the Select Defects list slides up.
  2. Tap every defect that applies. Defects are grouped and tagged by severity (Critical, Major, Minor, Cosmetic), and the list only shows defects valid for that part (car or remote).
  3. Tap Log Defects. The button stays disabled until at least one defect is ticked. A red flash and buzz confirm the fail was logged (failing is meant to feel like a negative signal).
ⓘ At least one defect is required

You cannot log a fail with no defect selected. Recording the real defects is what feeds the quality reports and the workshop’s repair list, so pick all that apply rather than the first one you see.
