When a device wakes for a shift it asks who is using it. Which sign-in you see depends on whether a supervisor has already set the device up for today.

What it’s for
Identifying who is on the device.
Who
Operator Supervisor
Resets
Setup expires at 1 AM each day.
PIN
Held by supervisors.

Operator Sign In

If the device is already set up for the shift, you see Operator Sign In. Tap Select name manually, enter the supervisor PIN when prompted, then tap your name from the list. You will see “Welcome, [your name]” and the device moves on to the station setup screen.

Supervisor Sign In

If the device is not set up, the setup has expired, or it could not reach the server, it shows Supervisor Sign In with a red banner explaining why (for example “Shift setup expired”). A supervisor enters the Supervisor PIN, taps Confirm PIN, then picks their name. This leads into Device Setup.

ⓘ Why the PIN matters

The supervisor PIN protects who can set a device up and who can pick a name. A locked device at the start of a shift is normal and safe: it just means a supervisor needs to sign in and set the station before operators can scan.
