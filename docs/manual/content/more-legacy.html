Legacy Registration brings older, pre-system units into the system so they can be dispatched or stored. It has two modes depending on what you are doing with the unit.

Station
LEGACY_REG · shared.

Box EAN, or the car’s legacy QR.
Who
Operator Supervisor
Modes
Dispatch / Store.

The two modes

  • Dispatch: also pick the channel (Ecom / Retail) and which printer (D1 / D2). Scan the EAN on the box exterior; a fresh batch label is minted and printed. Stick it on the box.
  • Store: scan the legacy QR on the car base; the unit is registered into stock.
ⓘ Pick the mode first

Scanning before choosing Dispatch or Store gives “Select mode first”. If a unit is already in the system you will see “Already registered” with its status, which is just information, not an error.
