The flow

Scan a batch label (LOT-XXXXXXXX-E or -R). A green Handed Over tick confirms the unit is now in dispatch. That is the whole station: one scan per unit.

ⓘ If it rejects

“Invalid label, scan a PKG batch label” means the code was not a batch label (you may have a plain unit UPC or a box label). As a dispatch station it needs a live connection and your shift QR sign-in.
