Attendance is a standalone clock-in / clock-out terminal. Staff scan their ID card and the system records the time, working out automatically whether they are clocking in or out.

The flow

  1. Scan your QR ID card (or tap “Type ID instead” and enter your employee number).
  2. Confirm. Your name and the action appear; tap Confirm (it cancels itself after a few seconds if you walk away).
  3. See the result: a green “Clocked In” or “Clocked Out” with your name and the time.
ⓘ One card, both directions

You do not choose in or out: the system clocks you in if you are not already in, and out if you are. Attendance is a deviceless utility, so it needs no station or line setup. It is the attendance card (the LOT-FACT-XXXX employee card) that is used here, which is separate from any station login.
